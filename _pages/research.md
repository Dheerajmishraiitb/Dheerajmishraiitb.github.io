## Research Interests

I work on making large language models more capable and more efficient after pre-training: reinforcement learning based post-training, self-improvement, and model merging. I am also interested in vision-language models, robot perception and physical AI, and the systems side of machine learning, including optimization and efficient inference.

**Keywords:** Robot Perception and Learning · Physical AI · Reinforcement Learning · RL Post-Training for LLMs · Vision-Language Models · Systems for ML · Optimization · Efficient Inference

## Current Research

**Recursive Self-Improving Large Language Models via In-Context Learning (ICL) and Co-evolving U-OPSD**<br>
*MTech Thesis, Stochastic Systems Lab, IIT Bombay (2026 – present)*<br>
Advisor: Prof. Nikhil Karamchandani · Co-Advisor: Prof. N. Hemachandra<br>
Computing facility: PARAM Rudra supercomputer, IIT Bombay (National Supercomputing Mission)<br>
Investigating RL-based post-training methods to improve reasoning and alignment in LLMs, including PPO, GRPO and preference optimization, along with reward modeling and reasoning-oriented evaluation.

**Does RL-Induced Reasoning Survive Merging?**<br>
*BharatGen (Aug 2026 – present)*<br>
Advisor: Prof. Ganesh Ramakrishnan<br>
Investigating whether reasoning skills learned through RL with verifiable rewards survive model merging, comparing post-hoc task-vector merging with merging during distributed RL training (DiLoCo-style) on Qwen2.5 models from 0.5B to 7B. Built and operate a fault-tolerant multi-GPU experiment pipeline on the PARAM Rudra supercomputer (National Supercomputing Mission), covering hundreds of RL training, merging and evaluation jobs.

## Past Research

**Automatic Speech Recognition for Low-Resource Languages**<br>
*Research Intern, Max Planck Institute for Psycholinguistics, Netherlands (Summer 2026)*<br>
Guide: Dr. Arvind Kumar<br>
Developed and evaluated low-resource ASR models with Wav2Vec 2.0 and Whisper, applying transfer learning on a Mongolian speech dataset and analysing speech prosody.

**Region-Guided CT Report Generation with Merlin VLM**<br>
*MeDAL Lab, IIT Bombay (Spring 2026)*<br>
Advisor: Prof. Amit Sethi<br>
Combined a CT-pretrained vision-language model with cross-attention for region-aware clinical report generation, trained on the 1.3 TB RadGenome-ChestCT dataset. Improved BLEU-4 to 29.42 (+18.3%) and reached 0.598 region F1.

**Gait Recognition: From Basics to Recent Advances in Deep Learning & Gen AI Era**<br>
*MTech Seminar, IIT Bombay (Spring 2026)*<br>
Guide: Prof. Subhasis Chaudhuri<br>
A survey of gait recognition across deep learning, large vision models, 3D point clouds and diffusion models, comparing RGB, silhouette, skeleton and LiDAR representations.
